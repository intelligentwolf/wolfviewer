Dancing wolf for the login loading box (llprogressview.cpp drawDancingWolf).

Model: "Wolf" by Quaternius — CC0 1.0 (public domain), no attribution required.
  https://poly.pizza/m/P1gU3Qkr9r
Dance and colouring (timber coat #7a6655, cream #e9dcc4 — the model itself is grey) keyed on its rig
  and rendered in Blender 5.2 for WolfViewer, 2026-09-28
  (source scripts: ~/wolfviewer-releases/loading-wolf/dance.py on the build machine).
32 frames, 256x256 RGBA, played at 24 fps in a loop.
