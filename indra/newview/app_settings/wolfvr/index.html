WolfViewer VR

The picture comes from WolfViewer on your PC, over your home network.

Connection

Connecting to WolfViewer…

Waiting for you to press Allow on your PC.

Your PC is asking "A headset wants to connect". Press Allow there (you can lift the headset to see the screen). This page carries on by itself, and you only do this once for this headset.

Tip: bookmark this page, so next time you can open it from your bookmarks instead of typing.

Preview

Drag the picture to turn your head here on the page; in the headset, just look around.

Screen size:

Right controller: point at the screen; trigger = click, grip or B = right-click, stick up/down = scroll (pointing away from the screen: screen size), stick left/right = turn. Left controller: stick = walk (all the way = run), X = jump / fly up, grip = fly down, menu = screen back in front of you (hold for the numbers), Y = keyboard, stick click = VR 3D Mode (when it is offered).

Numbers

These show how quickly the picture reaches you. They also show inside VR (press the menu button on the left controller).
