Screen size:

Right controller: point at the screen; trigger = click, grip or B = right-click, stick up/down = scroll (pointing away from the screen: screen size), stick left/right = turn. To stretch the screen, point at it and pull one of the round corner handles with the trigger. The button under the screen turns the theatre round you on or off. Left controller: stick = walk (all the way = run), X = jump / fly up, grip = fly down, menu = screen back in front of you (hold for the numbers), Y = keyboard, stick click = VR 3D Mode (when it is offered).

Numbers

These show how quickly the picture reaches you. They also show inside VR (press the menu button on the left controller).
