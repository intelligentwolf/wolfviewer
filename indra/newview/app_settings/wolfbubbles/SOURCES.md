# WolfViewer bubble puffs: sources and licences

The recording here was dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 needs no attribution. We credit the
recordist anyway, with thanks.

The puffs were cut, filtered and encoded by
`region-terrain-work/swim-bubbles-2026-11-02/tools/build_sounds.py` in the firestorm repository.
That script is the exact recipe: excerpt times, filters, loudness and encoding. WolfStorm ships the
same three files (`wolfstorm/assets/sounds/bubbles/`).
